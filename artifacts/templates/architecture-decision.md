# Saffna Architecture Decision Template

Copy into `artifacts/architecture-decisions/` and replace this H1 with `ADR-NNNN: Decision Title`. Do not treat this template as an active decision.

**Status:** Proposed until approved  
**Decision owner:** Name the accountable Saffna owner  
**Date:** Record the decision date  
**Approval/task:** Record the named stage and direct request or ClickUp task ID/link

## Context

State the Saffna problem, confirmed constraints, affected runtime surfaces, and source classification. Separate owner-approved facts from dated observations, assumptions, and vendor claims.

## Decision

State the precise choice, its owning deployable or artifact, and any compatibility-sensitive technical identifiers.

## Alternatives Considered

For each relevant alternative, record benefits, costs/risks, and why it was not chosen. Do not invent an option merely to fill this section.

## Consequences

Record positive effects, trade-offs, and future constraints for the Laravel web app, API, PHP business layer, SQLite, or possible mobile consumer as applicable.

## Cross-Surface Impact

Check business rules, data/migrations, inbound/outbound contracts, security/privacy, Arabic/English UI, environments/operations, terminology, and task mapping. Mark non-applicable concerns with a reason.

## Validation And Reversal

Describe approved evidence, known limits, and how a later decision would supersede or reverse this one.

## Related Artifacts And Provenance

Link the governing rules, contracts, data and design artifacts, owner statement, and task or stage approval. Never include secrets.

## Supersession

- Supersedes: None until a prior decision is identified.
- Superseded by: None until a later approved decision exists.
